# Ride

Operate. A self-coached rider, on the bike, in ordinary room light. Job: hold the current watts against the plan and stop the ride. One rider, one machine.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Live watts are a roadside stone, and the power chart is the road they stand on. Refuse the dark trainer dashboard and a number that replaces the plan.

OWN-WORLD: Daylight stone `#f3f4f5`, white panels, ink `#161616` rules and type, one distance mark `#c45c12`. Archivo for the interface, Archivo Black for the watt numeral. Sharp edges. The mark is the chart playhead and the time left, nowhere else.

STORY: The rider sees the plan, the watts, and how long until the next step, then stops when the work is done. Tabs, devices, history, calendar, and profile keep their jobs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Tabs across the top. The power profile and the heart-rate / cadence strip stay above the numeral, full width, zones and series intact. The watts are the painted figure under that chart. Time left carries the orange mark. Stop is an ordinary button. Signature: the playhead is that same mark, moving along the chart.
