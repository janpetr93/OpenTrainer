# Milestone keeps the charts

## Context

The Ride redesign locked **The Milestone**: live watts painted like a roadside stone, one orange distance mark, cool daylight ground. The decision sketch showed that number and a step list, and left out the charts the ride is actually read from.

## Decision

Keep every existing chart. On Ride, the planned power profile (zones, live cursor, achieved and meter series) and the heart-rate / cadence strip stay in the first viewport. History session charts, calendar plan previews, and the Profile mean-maximal curve stay too.

The Milestone supplies type, the stone ground, the painted watt numeral, and the one orange mark (the chart playhead and the time left). It does not replace a chart with a step list.

## Alternatives considered

- **Stone numeral and step list only** — what the sketch showed. Drops the plan the rider follows.
- **Leave the charts in the Paper Cockpit palette** — the number would belong to a different screen than the plot.

## Consequences

- Chart structure, zone bands, and series meaning are unchanged.
- The playhead is the world's single orange mark. Structural color is ink, not cobalt.
