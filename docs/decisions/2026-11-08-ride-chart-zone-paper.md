# Ride chart zones are paper tints

## Context

Coggan zone bands on the Ride chart were 15–18% washes on a gray gradient, with a gray fill under the plan line and a heavy white veil ahead of the playhead. From the bike the background read as one muddy field.

## Decision

Zone bands are flat paper tints of the same hues (gray, blue, green, yellow, sand, red, violet). The chart field is white. The area under the plan line is clear so it does not gray the bands. The ahead veil is light enough that zones past the playhead still read. The playhead stays the only full-strength orange; Z5 is a sand tint, not that orange.

## Alternatives considered

- **Stronger versions of the old transparent washes** — still muddy once stacked on the gray field and the plan fill.
- **One ink ramp, no hue** — zones would not be nameable by color, which is how a rider reads them.

## Consequences

- History charts are unchanged; they do not draw zone bands.
- Legend swatches use the same hues at full strength.
